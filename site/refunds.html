Cancellations & refunds

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Cancel a membership

You may cancel a recurring Path Pooja membership at any time before its next renewal. After cancellation, you retain access to paid membership features until the end of the current paid billing period. No further renewal payment will be taken.

Refunds

Membership fees pay for access during the billing period. Refund requests for duplicate, unauthorised or incorrectly charged payments are reviewed case by case. Contact support promptly with your payment ID and the email used in Path Pooja so we can investigate.
